Privacy

Privacy policy

Most OpenToolbox processing occurs locally in your browser.

Data processing

Text, uploaded images and calculator inputs are not sent to an OpenToolbox application server. GitHub Pages may process standard hosting logs under GitHub's own policies.

External services

The currency converter requests reference exchange-rate data from the Frankfurter API. The QR code page loads the open-source QRCode.js library from jsDelivr. PayPal is only contacted when a visitor selects the donation link.

Local storage

The site may save the selected appearance and favourite-tool list in browser local storage. Clear the site's browser data to remove those preferences.
