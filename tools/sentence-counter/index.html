Purpose

What is the Sentence Counter for?

The Sentence Counter helps you count sentences, questions, exclamations and paragraphs. It is designed for quick everyday use, with a focused interface that keeps the inputs, options and result together.

Common ways to use this tool

  • Measure sentence and paragraph totals.
  • Count questions and exclamations.
  • Review sentence density in articles or scripts.

How to use the Sentence Counter

  1. 1
    Paste the content you want to analyse.
  2. 2
    Review sentence, question, exclamation and paragraph counts.
  3. 3
    Adjust punctuation if a sentence is not counted as expected.
  4. 4
    Use the totals to review structure and readability.

Understanding the result

Measure sentence and paragraph totals. Review the displayed values or generated output, then adjust the inputs to compare another result. Copy or download controls are provided when the tool creates reusable content or an image.

Important: Counts and transformations depend on the entered text and the browser rules used by the tool.

Privacy

OpenToolbox does not require a login. This tool is designed to process its inputs in your browser whenever possible. The currency converter requests external reference rates, and the QR generator loads its rendering library online; those exceptions are explained in the privacy page.
