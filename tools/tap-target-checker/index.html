Purpose

What is the Tap Target Size Checker for?

Review declared interactive control sizes against touch guidance. It is designed for quick everyday work while keeping the inputs, controls and result together.

How to use the Tap Target Size Checker

  1. 1
    Enter or upload the information requested by the tool.
  2. 2
    Choose the relevant options, units or output format.
  3. 3
    Select the main action button or press Ctrl/Command + Enter.
  4. 4
    Review the result, then copy, download or adjust the inputs.

Privacy and local storage

OpenToolbox does not require a login. Most processing happens inside your browser. Favourites, history, preferences and optional saved form values use local storage on your device and can be exported or deleted from My Toolbox.

Accessibility guidance

Every automated result still needs human review when it affects accessibility, finance, safety, legal work or important documents. Use the result as a practical aid rather than a final professional decision.
