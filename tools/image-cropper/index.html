Purpose

What is the Image Cropper for?

The Image Cropper helps you crop an image by pixel coordinates and dimensions. It is designed for quick everyday use, with a focused interface that keeps the inputs, options and result together.

Common ways to use this tool

  • Crop a selected pixel region.
  • Remove unwanted edges or empty space.
  • Download only the chosen area.

How to use the Image Cropper

  1. 1
    Upload the image you want to crop.
  2. 2
    Enter the crop position and dimensions in pixels.
  3. 3
    Preview the selected region.
  4. 4
    Download the cropped result to your device.

Understanding the result

Crop a selected pixel region. Review the displayed values or generated output, then adjust the inputs to compare another result. Copy or download controls are provided when the tool creates reusable content or an image.

Important: Files are processed in the browser where possible. Very large images can be limited by device memory.

Privacy

OpenToolbox does not require a login. This tool is designed to process its inputs in your browser whenever possible. The currency converter requests external reference rates, and the QR generator loads its rendering library online; those exceptions are explained in the privacy page.
