Purpose

What is the Favicon Generator for?

The Favicon Generator helps you create a square PNG favicon from an uploaded image. It is designed for quick everyday use, with a focused interface that keeps the inputs, options and result together.

Common ways to use this tool

  • Turn an uploaded image into a square favicon.
  • Preview a small browser-icon version.
  • Download a PNG for a website project.

How to use the Favicon Generator

  1. 1
    Upload a clear square or near-square source image.
  2. 2
    Choose the favicon size offered by the tool.
  3. 3
    Generate and preview the small icon.
  4. 4
    Download the PNG and add it to your website project.

Understanding the result

Turn an uploaded image into a square favicon. Review the displayed values or generated output, then adjust the inputs to compare another result. Copy or download controls are provided when the tool creates reusable content or an image.

Important: Some platforms require additional favicon formats or manifest settings beyond a single PNG.

Privacy

OpenToolbox does not require a login. This tool is designed to process its inputs in your browser whenever possible. The currency converter requests external reference rates, and the QR generator loads its rendering library online; those exceptions are explained in the privacy page.
