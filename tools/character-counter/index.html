Purpose

What is the Character Counter for?

The Character Counter helps you count characters with and without spaces, words and lines. It is designed for quick everyday use, with a focused interface that keeps the inputs, options and result together.

Common ways to use this tool

  • Check limits for titles, descriptions and forms.
  • Compare counts with and without spaces.
  • Measure line and word totals at the same time.

How to use the Character Counter

  1. 1
    Paste or type the text you need to measure.
  2. 2
    Review characters with spaces, characters without spaces, words and lines.
  3. 3
    Edit the text while the counters update live.
  4. 4
    Use the final count against the limit for your platform or form.

Understanding the result

Check limits for titles, descriptions and forms. Review the displayed values or generated output, then adjust the inputs to compare another result. Copy or download controls are provided when the tool creates reusable content or an image.

Important: Counts and transformations depend on the entered text and the browser rules used by the tool.

Privacy

OpenToolbox does not require a login. This tool is designed to process its inputs in your browser whenever possible. The currency converter requests external reference rates, and the QR generator loads its rendering library online; those exceptions are explained in the privacy page.
